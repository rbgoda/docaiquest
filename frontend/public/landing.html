Compliance by design

Privacy isn't a policy. It's the architecture.

PII-safe AI

Personal identifiers (passport, NRIC, account #, DOB, email, phone) are redacted before any data reaches a language model, and every AI call is written to an audit ledger.

GDPR (EU)

You are both controller and custodian. Data minimization and the right to erasure are structural — delete your Drive folder and it's gone. No vendor copy, no cross-border transfer.

PDPA (SEA)

Consent, purpose limitation, and protection are met by on-demand, in-memory processing where the data of record never resides with the vendor.
