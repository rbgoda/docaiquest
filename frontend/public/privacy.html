Privacy Policy

DocAIQuest helps you process your documents and ask questions about them with AI. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the controls you have. Questions: support@docaiq.jicama.tech.

1. Information we collect

  • Account: your name and email (via email/password sign-up or Google sign-in).
  • Your documents: files you upload or that you connect from Google Drive, and data we derive from them (extracted text, summaries, document types, search embeddings, chat history).
  • Usage: basic operational logs (requests, errors, AI usage counts for plan limits).

2. How we use it

Solely to provide the service: to process your documents (parse, classify, extract fields), power search and AI chat, enforce plan limits, and keep the service secure and reliable. We do not sell your data or use your document content to train our own models.

3. Google user data & Google Drive

When you connect Google Drive, DocAIQuest requests the minimum scope drive.file — access only to files the app creates or that you explicitly open with it. We do not request access to your entire Drive. Your original files remain in your Google Drive.

Limited Use disclosure. DocAIQuest's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not transfer or use Google user data for advertising, and we do not allow humans to read it except with your consent, for security, or as required by law.

4. AI sub-processors

To generate summaries, extractions, and chat answers, relevant excerpts of your documents are sent to third-party AI providers (e.g. Anthropic, Google, and OpenRouter-routed models) strictly to produce your result. Before content is sent, we apply PII redaction so personal identifiers are tokenized. These providers process the data on our behalf and do not use it to train their models under our configuration.

5. Personal data protection (PII)

Detected personal identifiers are tokenized and protected at rest, and redacted before being sent to AI providers. You can reveal protected values within your own account.

6. Your rights (GDPR / PDPA)

You can export all your data and delete your account and associated data at any time from your account settings. To exercise any data-subject right, use the in-app controls or contact support@docaiq.jicama.tech.

7. Retention

We retain your account and document-derived data while your account is active. When you delete a document or your account, the associated server-side data is removed. Files stored in your own Google Drive remain under your control.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and access is scoped per user. Optional client-side encryption is available for files DocAIQuest stores in your Drive.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected here with a new date.
